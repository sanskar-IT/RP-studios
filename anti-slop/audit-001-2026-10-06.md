### 2. R-31, decorative separator banners

48 lines of `# --- Label --------------------------` across 12 files. The rule the skill names is "Decorative Separators": the decoration is the message, and a label wrapped in `-` signals generator output without adding information.

```

```

These are not all empty labels. `services/evaluation/performer.py:254,321,428,527,631,728,890,985,1069` name scenarios A through J and do carry information, and `services/performer/performer.py:78,236,367,570,705,805,932,1208` name real sections of a 1223-line file. The pattern the rule rejects is the decoration itself: a plain `# Reading the result` tells the reader the same thing without the 60 dashes.

Your call on scope. The conservative fix is the dashes only. The stricter fix also removes the labels that name nothing the code does not already show.
